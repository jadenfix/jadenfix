## Research & Achievements
- **Award-Winning Thesis:**  
  My undergraduate thesis, *Predictive Stochastic Modeling for Financial Markets*, earned the **Adam Smith Award** for outstanding economic research. It explores how financial and macroeconomic indicators can predict bear markets in the S&P 500 using advanced econometric and machine learning techniques.  
  📄 [Read the full thesis](https://github.com/jadenfix/Predictive-Stochastic-Modeling-for-Financial-Markets-/blob/main/Predictive_Stochastic_Modeling_for_Financial_Markets.pdf)

- **Comprehensive Quantitative Analysis:**  
  My research integrates Bayesian inference, time-series econometrics, and machine learning-inspired forecasting to provide deep insights into market downturns and risk management strategies.
